## Legal Positioning

### Regulatory Classification
- **Paper trading (now through T33):** No regulatory exposure — simulated execution only
- **Live trading with own capital ($2k):** Personal investment account — no broker-dealer registration required in the US for trading your own capital
- **SaaS licensing:** Does not constitute investment advice if:
  - Product includes clear disclaimers ("not financial advice")
  - Users are executing their own trades
  - TradeSwarm does not hold client funds
- **Islamic finance compliance:** DJIM methodology is industry standard — not legally regulated but academically and commercially recognized

### Risk Disclosures Required
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- Algorithmic trading involves substantial risk of loss
- Halal screening is based on DJIM methodology — not a fatwa or formal religious ruling
- Human scholar review is required before representing compliance to Islamic institutions

### Alif Positioning
- TradeSwarm-Halal is an Islamic tech product, not a financial advisor
- TruthCal™ receipts provide audit trail for scholar review
- Q6 (OPEN): Alif scholar review requirement — verify before T31 design
